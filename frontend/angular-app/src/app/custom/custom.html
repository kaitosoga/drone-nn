Custom Controller

Design your custom thrust equations by building expressions with the available operations and variables below.


Please read the Explanation for help:
Welcome to your own interface for crafting controllers. If you've played the game, you've seen the two thrusters moving the drone left, right, up, and down. In the following interface, you can build functions for both thrusters (left & right). As an input you can use various state variables, i.e., the drone's velocity, acceleration, angle, and it's coordintes relative to the checkpoints (the vector from your drone to the next checkpoint.)

Using the operations available, you compose symmetrical (or unsymmetrical) equations for each thrust, based on the environment's state (you don't need to use them all) by clicking the operation / variable you need, draging them within your equation, and removing them (double click) if you want to get rid of them.

To make a good controller, you should look at the gameplay of a drone to observe the physics, and experiment with equations.
