Control Guide To steer, use the left, right, up arrows, or the "a", "d", "w" keys.
Start by clicking the up/w key, to use both thrusts for upward thrust.
Then use left,right / a,d keys to make the left or right thrust a bit stronger.
This will move the drone in the opposite direction.
To move downward, just release the thrusts, but don't wait to long to catch it!
To go very fast, click all three keys at once.

If you don't have a keyboard, the "Touch" options display your buttons.
To hide/display the options menu, you can always click "☰ Options"

{{sRat}} Options Guide Choose the player options in the first row of buttons
You have to choose exactly two players, unless you are in training mode, where scores won't be saved to your account.
If one of the players is an AI, you also have to choose a level, determining the skill of the AI.
If you chose to challenge another player's custom controller from the leaderboard, it will be indicated and you have to choose "Custom" to battle it.
You can pause, resume, quit, and start the game with the corresponding buttons.
