AI Pilots

Welcome to AI Pilots

AI Pilots is a 2D game playing in space where the objective is to steer your drone carefully from checkpoint to checkpoint in limited amount of time. At the same time, you compete against an AI - it ahs the same goal as you, and you have one minute to collect more checkpoints than the AI opponent.

You can control your drone with arrow keys or awd, where each key activate a thrust on the drone when clicked - because the drone follows standard physics, you will have to maneuver against gravity, momentum, and not loose control! This project is not focused just on games - the purpose of AI Pilots is to demonstrate the differences between human actors and AI bots in interactive, visual, and hands-on ways, and to display the inner workings of the AI (an FFN) in real time.

You don't know what an FFN is? That's where the inspect section will help you, by explaining the components and the intuition behind what the AI (FFN) does without the abstract maths.

In addition, there are many more features like visualising the AI in real time as it plays the game, an easy interface for building your own, custom controller and let it play against the AI, yourself, or even others, a selection of skins to give your own drone, the creation of an account without an email to save all your work, and a public leaderboard both for humans and their custom controllers.

Game

Upon a countdown of 3 seconds, you have one minute to move your drone from checkpoitn to checkpoint as fast as possible. You can play against an AI of three different levels, and your own custom controller, and even against the controllers of other players.

Whether you just want to game or compare your skills against bots, here you can do both.

This example image shows the drones in action. The lasers visualised the activated thrusts, and the stars are your checkpoints. The drone with a tail track is yous, the other one the opponent.

The AI & Inspect Mode

The AI is a so-called FFN, which will be explained to you in the Inspect section. It serves the goal of visualising what goes on inside the AI, and of explaining its inner working with intuitive words and analogies.

Inside the Inspect section you can change the inputs that the AI takes manually to interact with and see the reaction of the AI.

When you are inside the Game section, you can click "Inspect" to visualised the AI's neurons in ral time.

Custom Controllers

Instead of playing against AI yourself, you can try to build a controller yourself to beat the AI.

The interace allows you to use various functions and the state variables (e.g., veocity on the Y axis, acceleration, angle, cooridnates of the next checkpoint, etc...) - you just drag&drop them to build the custom controller.

This allows you to experiment with control strategies, programming, and physics - things that are usually outside of the app.

Accounts

You should create an account, for which you don't need an email, so your top scores and custom controllers will be saved. Remember your password!

Public Leaderboards and Shared Controllers

There are two public leaderboards for humans and their custom controllers.
If you have an account, your scores will automatically be considered for the leaderboard - only the top 10 scores make it, and are displayed with your profile name. For the top 10 custom controllers, there is also the option to play against these controllers - just click "challenge" and select the custom option in the Game section.

Skins

Regardless of your top score you can choose any skin for your drone. This is because your skin won't be visible to others, so the top score doesn't matter or determine your skin.
